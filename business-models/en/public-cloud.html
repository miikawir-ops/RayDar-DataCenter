Public cloud

Public cloud: AI Ltd buys computing capacity as a service over the internet
SITUATION

AI Ltd has just been founded. It needs to test and train its first models right away, its funding is limited, and its needs can't be forecast yet.

DECISION

AI Ltd opens an account with a hyperscaler's cloud and starts GPU virtual machines as needed.

REAL-WORLD COMPARISON

Microsoft's Azure has been OpenAI's main cloud since 2016. Exclusivity began in 2019; since April 2026 OpenAI can offer its products on other clouds too, but Azure remains its primary cloud.

CONTRACT

Cloud services agreement

WHAT AI LTD BUYS
A service. AI Ltd buys capacity as it uses it and owns no equipment or premises.
PARTIES
AI Ltd and the cloud service provider (CSP)
CONTRACT STRUCTURE
A standard customer agreement and service-specific terms. Large customers have a negotiated enterprise agreement
WHAT IS DELIVERED
GPU virtual machines, storage, networking and ready platform services, not a specific physical server
WHAT IS PAID FOR
By use: GPU hours, data stored and transferred. A discount for committing to a level of use for 1–3 years (committed use)
TERM
No minimum term; commitment discounts 1–3 years
SLA
Availability per service, typically 99.9–99.99%. Credits for shortfalls (service credits)
NOT INCLUDED
Protecting AI Ltd's applications and data, which AI Ltd is responsible for itself (shared responsibility model)
