Own data center

On-premise / self-build: AI Ltd builds, owns and operates its own data center
SITUATION

AI Ltd needs hundreds of megawatts of computing capacity and wants to control the whole chain. Others build too slowly, and leasing no longer adds flexibility.

DECISION

AI Ltd secures the land and a grid connection, has the data center built by contractors and operates it itself.

REAL-WORLD COMPARISON

xAI built its own Colossus data center in Memphis. Google owns and operates its Hamina data center itself.

CONTRACT

Several contracts, no single main contract

WHAT AI LTD BUYS
No ready capacity: AI Ltd builds and owns it itself. It buys construction, equipment and electricity.
PARTIES
AI Ltd and several suppliers: contractors, equipment suppliers, electricity companies and grid operators
KEY CONTRACTS
A construction contract (e.g. EPC), equipment supply, the grid connection and an electricity contract (for large players often also a PPA), service and maintenance
WHAT IS PROCURED
The land, the building, the power and cooling systems, the servers and their maintenance
WHAT IS PAID FOR
Investment (capex) in the building and equipment, and ongoing operating costs (opex): electricity, maintenance and staff
TERM
The building lasts decades, the servers a few years
PERMITS
Zoning, a building permit and environmental permits from the municipality and the authorities
NOT INCLUDED
A lease, unless AI Ltd later does a sale-leaseback
