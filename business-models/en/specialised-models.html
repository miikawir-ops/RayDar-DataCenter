Specialized models

Other models in the industry, tied to location, revenue model, demand, ownership or extra revenue
LOCATION

Edge data centers

Small, distributed data centers close to users. The value comes from low latency: 5G, IoT and content delivery.

RELATES TO
Stage 3 (retail colocation)
E.G.
EdgeConneX, Vapor IO
REVENUE MODEL

Interconnection

A carrier-neutral data center as the meeting point of networks. Revenue comes from cross-connects and the ecosystem, not only from space.

RELATES TO
Stage 3 (retail colocation)
E.G.
Equinix, Digital Realty; in Finland, the FICIX internet exchanges
DEMAND

Cloud giants' leased capacity

AWS, Microsoft and Google lease part of their capacity from colocation companies and GPU clouds, alongside building their own. The largest source of wholesale demand (leased hyperscale).

RELATES TO
Stages 2 and 4
E.G.
Microsoft's capacity contracts with Nebius and CoreWeave
OWNERSHIP AND FINANCING

Development and financing models

Build-to-suit (built for one tenant), sale-leaseback (sale and leaseback), data center REITs and infrastructure funds' joint ventures decide who owns the property.

RELATES TO
Stages 4 and 5; more on the Financing models page
E.G.
Blackstone bought QTS (2021); Equinix and Digital Realty are REITs
EXTRA REVENUE

Selling waste heat

The servers' heat is sold to the district heating (kaukolämpö) network. The extra revenue improves profitability and local acceptance.

RELATES TO
The data center's owner or operator
E.G.
Nebius in Mäntsälä since 2015; Microsoft and Fortum's agreement on future data centers
PUBLIC SECTOR

Public computing infrastructure

Supercomputers funded by governments and the EU, whose computing time is granted to research and companies through calls for applications.

RELATES TO
An alternative to stages 1–2
E.G.
LUMI in Kajaani (EuroHPC); Silo AI trained its Poro and Viking models on LUMI
