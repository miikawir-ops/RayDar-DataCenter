WHAT STARGATE IS

Stargate is an AI data center project in the United States by OpenAI and its partners, and the Abilene campus in Texas is its first site. Crusoe develops the Abilene campus and owns it together with Blue Owl. Oracle has leased the campus for 15 years, operates the servers and sells the computing capacity to OpenAI. xAI held talks with Oracle on a large cloud deal in 2024, but in the end built its own Colossus data center in Memphis. According to news reports, OpenAI and Oracle dropped an expansion in spring 2026, and the neighboring site of several hundred megawatts is leased by Microsoft.
What the comparison teaches

The same chain is a different model from a different point of view: for Oracle, Abilene is a wholesale lease (stage 4); for OpenAI, it is purchased capacity reserved for its own use (stage 2).

The end user sees the same kind of service. Behind it, though, there can be one company or four, each owning different assets, carrying different risks and getting its own share of the revenue, possibly in different countries.
