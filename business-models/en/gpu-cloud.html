GPU cloud

GPU cloud / neocloud: AI Ltd reserves GPU capacity for its own use from a cloud company specialized in AI computing
SITUATION

AI Ltd's models are in production, and its GPU use is continuous. The public cloud's hourly price gets expensive, and capacity isn't always available when it's needed.

DECISION

AI Ltd signs a three-year contract with a GPU cloud, reserving a fixed amount of GPU capacity for its own use.

REAL-WORLD COMPARISON

In September 2025 Microsoft agreed with Nebius on about $17.4 billion of GPU capacity over five years. OpenAI has similar contracts with CoreWeave.

CONTRACT

Capacity agreement

WHAT AI LTD BUYS
A service, but AI Ltd commits to paying for the reserved capacity for the whole contract term.
PARTIES
AI Ltd and the GPU cloud
CONTRACT STRUCTURE
A framework agreement and a capacity order, e.g. a set number of GPUs in a specific data center
WHAT IS DELIVERED
GPU servers or a cluster reserved for its own use, networking, storage and maintenance
WHAT IS PAID FOR
A fixed price per GPU hour or per month for the whole contract term. Paid whether used or not (take-or-pay). Often partly in advance
TERM
Typically 1–5 years
SLA
Capacity availability and performance. Credits for shortfalls (service credits)
NOT INCLUDED
AI Ltd's models, software or data
