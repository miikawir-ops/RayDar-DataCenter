Wholesale colocation

Colocation at wholesale scale: AI Ltd leases a whole data hall or building on a long contract
SITUATION

AI Ltd's computing needs have grown to tens of megawatts. Retail colocation can't supply enough space and power in one place, and the unit price is high.

DECISION

AI Ltd leases a whole data hall on a ten-year contract and fits it with its own servers.

REAL-WORLD COMPARISON

Oracle leased the Abilene, Texas campus of OpenAI's Stargate project from Crusoe and Blue Owl for 15 years and sells the computing capacity to OpenAI.

CONTRACT

Wholesale lease

WHAT AI LTD BUYS
The right to use the space, on a long lease. The contract is close to a property lease.
PARTIES
AI Ltd (tenant) and the data center operator (landlord)
CONTRACT STRUCTURE
A long lease, similar to a property lease. Often with expansion and renewal options
WHAT IS DELIVERED
A data hall or building and reserved power (MW). Turnkey: the power and cooling systems too. Powered shell: only the shell and the grid connection
WHAT IS PAID FOR
A fixed rent for reserved power (€/kW per month) for the whole term, often with annual increases (escalator). Electricity separately
TERM
Typically 5–15 years, often with renewal options
SLA
At turnkey sites, availability of power and cooling; at powered shell sites, narrow
NOT INCLUDED
The servers, software or data, which are AI Ltd's
